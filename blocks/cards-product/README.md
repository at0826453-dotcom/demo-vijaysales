# cards-product

Custom **cards** block. Purpose: scrolling carousel of bordered white product cards with badges, rating, price/MRP and discount.

## Authoring (Document Authoring)

Model: `standalone`

Single block table. Content: one row per product; cell 1: product image; cell 2: badges, product name link, rating, price (strike the MRP with del), discount and extra lines (delivery, offers) as paragraphs.

No variations.
